The problem.
As a coffee reviewer, I kept hitting the same wall. I could taste the difference between two coffees but could not describe it beyond "this one tastes more like coffee." I had no words. Every free vocab app was built for language learners grinding flashcards, not for someone who wanted a curated set of words specific to what they actually care about.
The solution.
A free, fully offline vocabulary app you can load with any 365 words you want. One word a day. A puzzle to test it 14 days later. A quiz that only unlocks when you have actually struggled with enough words to make it worth taking. No flashcard grind, no streaks for show. Just the words you need, delivered at a pace that actually works.

How it works
Word of the Day
A new word daily from a 365-word curated list. Each entry includes definition, part of speech, pronunciation, example sentence, etymology, and difficulty tier. A home screen widget shows the current word without opening the app.
Daily Puzzle
A 4-choice multiple choice question built around the word from 14 days ago — giving you time to actually learn it before being tested. One attempt per day. Get it wrong and the word goes to your missed list for the quiz.
Weekly Quiz
Unlocks after you have missed 3 new words since the last session. Draws only from your personal missed words list. Words you get right are removed. Words you miss stay. 24-hour cooldown between sessions so it cannot be gamed.
Scoring System
Two-part score: a performance score weighted by word difficulty and accuracy, and a habit score tracking consistency over time with Duolingo-style decay for skipped days. Results compress into monthly and yearly averages.
Custom Word Lists
Import up to 365 of your own words. The app automatically enriches each one using the Merriam-Webster API at build time, adding definitions, etymology, and difficulty tiers. All words are baked into a JSON asset so the app runs fully offline after setup.
Stats Screen
Full progress breakdown: streak, accuracy ring chart built with CustomPainter, weekly activity dots, correct and assisted and wrong counts with an animated bar chart, and words to review before the next quiz unlocks.
